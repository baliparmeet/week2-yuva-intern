## Project Overview
This project focuses on understanding skincare customer satisfaction through ratings and written reviews. The Week 2 task involves identifying suitable data sources, evaluating their advantages and limitations, assessing initial data quality, and planning exploratory analysis.

## Primary Dataset
Sephora Products and Skincare Reviews — Kaggle

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/nadyinky/sephora-products-and-skincare-reviews

The working Excel dataset contains 49,977 rows and 19 columns, with an average rating of approximately 4.21 out of 5. Week 1 used a separate sample of 1,000 reviews, with an average rating of 4.1 out of 5.

## Other Potential Data Sources
- **Open Beauty Facts:** https://world.openbeautyfacts.org/
- **U.S. FDA cosmetic adverse-event data:** https://open.fda.gov/apis/cosmetic/event/
- **Google Trends:** https://trends.google.com/trends/
- **Sephora website:** https://www.sephora.com/

These sources may provide additional product details, ingredient information, reported safety concerns, search-interest patterns, and customer feedback.

## Planned Analysis
1. Calculate average ratings and rating distributions.
2. Identify common review topics such as hydration, softness, scent, irritation, and price.
3. Examine low-rated reviews for recurring concerns.
4. Explore associations between review sentiment and ratings.
5. Explore associations between product prices and ratings.
6. Present results using tables and visualizations.

## Data Preprocessing Plan
Check missing values, investigate potential duplicate reviews, validate ratings, inspect unusual prices, clean review text, and save a separate cleaned dataset.

## Limitations
Customer reviews may not represent every customer. Keyword matching is not full sentiment analysis, and observed associations do not establish causation. Additional data sources may have incomplete coverage or different definitions.
